# CDC Crosswalk — Early Growth Markers vs. CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early."

Date: 2026-11-19. Scope: due-diligence pass comparing our original-wording
corpus (`app/backend/child_development.py`) against the CDC milestone
checklists (2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 48, 60 months).

## Method

Each CDC checklist item was mapped to our five domains (social-emotional,
communication, thinking, gross motor, fine motor). Items we already covered in
equivalent wording were left alone (original wording is a copyright guardrail).
CDC items we did NOT cover were added in our own words. CDC's 30-month
checklist had no counterpart in our corpus at all, so the **30mo age group was
added**.

## Gaps filled (added to the corpus this pass)

- **4mo social**: chuckles (laughs) during play.
- **6mo social**: laughs out loud. **6mo communication**: blows raspberries.
- **9mo gross motor**: begins to crawl (or scoots/army-crawls).
- **12mo fine motor**: drinks from an open cup with help.
- **18mo thinking**: copies you doing chores (sweeping, wiping).
- **24mo social**: *notices when others are hurt or upset — pauses or looks
  sad when someone is crying* (owner-requested example); looks at your face to
  see how you react to a new situation. **24mo communication**: says at least
  50 words. **24mo fine motor**: uses both hands together (turns pages, opens
  doors, twists lids).
- **36mo social**: calms within about 10 minutes after you leave (drop-off).
  **36mo fine motor**: strings large beads/pasta; puts on some loose clothes.
- **48mo social**: pretends to be something else during play (teacher,
  superhero, dinosaur). **48mo fine motor**: pours and fills with little
  spilling; unbuttons buttons.
- **60mo fine motor**: buttons some buttons by themselves.
- **NEW 30mo age group**: pretend play with detail; follows routines and helps
  clean up; widening range of emotions; ~50 words; 2+ word phrases; points at
  books; both-hands coordination; sorting/matching; jumps; climbs; turns pages
  one at a time; takes off some clothes; early drawing strokes.

## Deliberate exclusions

- CDC's "often can't tell what's real and not real" (48mo) — a caution note
  about perception, not a skill to track.
- CDC wording that duplicates an existing corpus item with trivial variation.

## Guardrails unchanged

Original wording only (no CDC text reproduced verbatim); the non-diagnostic
banner stays pinned; the checklists are observation aids for parents, not
screens.
