# Developmental Checkpoints

> **What this is.** A plain-language, family-written guide to things children
> *often* begin to do at different ages, grouped by area of development, to help
> **you** notice and gently encourage your child's growth.
>
> **What this is NOT.** Not a test, a score, or a diagnosis — and this app is not
> a medical tool. Children develop at their own pace and reach these at different
> times; not having shown one yet is very often completely normal. Use these as
> friendly conversation starters with your pediatrician.
>
> **A gentle, always-true note:** each age is a **window, not a deadline.** If
> your child seems to be **losing a skill they once had**, or you have **any
> concern** about how they're growing, reach out to your pediatrician — that is
> exactly what they are there for.
>
> _Facts from public-domain references (MedlinePlus / U.S. National Library of
> Medicine; WHO motor-milestone windows), cross-checked with AAP. Wording is our
> own. For each item, mark what fits today — there is no "right" number._
>
> Mark key: **☐ not yet · ☐ starting to · ☐ seen it** (+ optional first-noticed date)

## ~2 Months

### Social & Emotional
- Begins to smile back when you smile at them (a true social smile). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Briefly calms when picked up, spoken to, or held. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Looks at your face with interest. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Makes soft cooing or gurgling sounds. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Turns toward or quiets at a familiar voice. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Startles or reacts to a sudden loud sound. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Watches your face and follows you with their eyes for a moment. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Begins to track a moving object side to side. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Lifts their head briefly during tummy time. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Makes smoother movements with arms and legs. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Hands are often unclenched rather than tightly fisted. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Brings a hand toward the mouth. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** lots of face-to-face talking and smiling; short, supervised tummy time.

## ~4 Months

### Social & Emotional
- Smiles on their own to get your attention. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Enjoys playing with people and may cry when play stops. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Copies some facial expressions. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Babbles with expression and takes "turns" making sounds with you. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Turns their head toward the sound of your voice. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Uses different cries for different needs. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Follows a moving toy from side to side with their eyes. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Looks at their own hands with interest. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Reaches for a toy they want. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Holds their head steady, without support, when held upright. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Pushes up onto elbows/forearms during tummy time. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- May roll from tummy to back. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Brings hands together and to the mouth. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Grasps a toy placed in their hand and shakes it. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** offer easy-to-grab rattles; respond back when they "talk."

## ~6 Months

### Social & Emotional
- Knows familiar faces and may begin to notice strangers. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Enjoys looking at themselves in a mirror. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Responds to others' emotions (smiles, frowns). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Takes turns making sounds; strings vowels together ("ah," "eh," "oh"). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Responds to their name being called. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Makes sounds to show pleasure or displeasure. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Looks around at nearby things with curiosity. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Brings things to the mouth to explore them. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Reaches for and grabs a toy they want. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Rolls over in both directions. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Begins to sit with a little support, then briefly on their own. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- When on tummy, pushes up on straight arms. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Moves a toy from one hand to the other. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Rakes small objects toward themselves. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** give safe objects of different textures to hold and mouth.

## ~9 Months

### Social & Emotional
- May be clingy or wary with unfamiliar people. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Has favorite toys and people. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Reacts to your tone of voice. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Understands "no." — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Babbles strings of sounds like "mamama," "babababa." — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Copies sounds and simple gestures. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Looks for a toy when it is hidden (early object permanence). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Watches the path of something as it falls. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Passes objects smoothly from hand to hand. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Sits well without support. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Gets into a sitting position by themselves. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Pulls up to stand and may begin to "cruise." — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Bangs two objects together. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Begins to pick up small items with thumb and finger. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** play hide-the-toy; name people and objects as you go.

---

## ~12 Months

### Social & Emotional
- Plays back-and-forth games like peekaboo or pat-a-cake. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Waves "bye-bye." — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Looks to a familiar adult and checks your face before trying something new. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Gives or shows you a toy just to share it, then often wants it back. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- **Points at something to ask for it or to show you.** — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Babbles in long strings that rise and fall like real talking. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Uses "mama" or "dada" for the *right* person. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Can follow a simple request, especially paired with a gesture. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Looks for a toy they watched you hide. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Looks where you point and shares attention on the same thing. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Drops or places an object into a container on purpose. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Copies a simple action they've seen you do. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- "Cruises" sideways holding on to furniture. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Stands alone for a moment when they let go. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- May take first steps _(first independent steps commonly emerge later in the second year)_. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Picks up a small item with a refined pincer grasp (thumb + one fingertip). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Points or pokes with one finger. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Finger-feeds themselves small, soft pieces of food. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** name what they point at; offer safe finger-foods and board books.

## ~18 Months

### Social & Emotional
- Points to show you something interesting. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Hands you a book when they want a story. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- May have tantrums and may cling to caregivers in new situations. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Ventures to explore while staying close to a familiar caregiver. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Says several single words. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Shakes head "no" and may say "no." — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Points to one body part when asked. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Follows a simple 1-step direction *without* a gesture. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Plays simple pretend, like feeding a doll. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Points to get your attention to something. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Scribbles on their own. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Walks on their own. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Begins to walk up steps with help. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Pulls toys while walking. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Drinks from a cup (with some spills). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Eats with a spoon, learning. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Stacks a couple of blocks. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** read together daily; name things they point to; offer a spoon at meals.

## ~24 Months (2 years)

### Social & Emotional
- Copies others, especially adults and older children. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Gets excited around other children. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Plays alongside other children rather than directly with them. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Says two-word phrases ("more milk," "go outside"). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Points to things in a book when named. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Follows simple two-step instructions. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Familiar people can understand much of their speech. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Finds an object even after seeing it hidden under a cover. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Fills and dumps containers over and over. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Plays make-believe. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Runs. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Kicks a ball. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Walks up and down a few stairs holding on. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Builds a small tower of several blocks. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Scribbles and may imitate a straight line. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** expand their words ("milk" → "more cold milk"); offer stacking and sorting toys.

## ~36 Months (3 years)

### Social & Emotional
- Takes turns in simple games (with reminders). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Shows concern when a playmate is upset. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Separates from caregivers more easily. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Speaks in short sentences of 3+ words. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Says their first name and age when asked. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Strangers can understand most of their speech. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Asks "why?" and other simple questions. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Works a toy with buttons, levers, or moving parts. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Does simple puzzles (3–4 pieces). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Plays pretend with dolls, animals, or people. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Climbs well and runs easily. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Walks up stairs with one foot per step. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Pedals a tricycle. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Turns book pages one at a time. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Copies a circle with a crayon. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Builds a taller tower of blocks. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** give puzzles and crayons; answer their "why" questions and ask your own.

## ~48 Months (4 years)

### Social & Emotional
- Enjoys cooperative play and make-believe with other children. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Would rather play *with* other children than alone. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Talks about what they like and are interested in. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Tells a simple story or recounts part of their day. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Speaks clearly enough for strangers to understand. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Knows some basic grammar rules ("he," "she"). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Sings a song or says a short poem from memory. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Names a few colors and some numbers. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Understands the idea of counting. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Begins to grasp time (morning, night, soon). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Hops and stands on one foot for a few seconds. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Catches a bounced ball most of the time. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Draws a person with a few body parts (roughly two to four). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Uses child-safe scissors with help. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Copies simple shapes like a plus sign and a few uppercase letters. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** play counting and color games; give child-safe scissors and drawing time.

## ~60 Months (5 years)

### Social & Emotional
- Seeks approval from and imitates friends. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Follows rules and takes turns in games. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Shows more independence and may do simple chores. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Communication & Language
- Speaks clearly in full sentences and holds a conversation. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Tells a simple story with a beginning, middle, and end. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Uses future tense ("we will go"). — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Says their full name and address with help. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Thinking & Problem-Solving
- Counts 10 or more things. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Names several colors and some letters/numbers. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Knows about everyday things like money and food. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Gross Motor
- Hops on one foot and may skip. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Does a somersault or swings and climbs with confidence. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

### Fine Motor
- Draws a person with at least 6 body parts. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Prints some letters and numbers. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it
- Uses a fork and spoon well. — ☐ not yet ☐ starting ☐ seen it

**What helps:** encourage drawing and early letters; give small responsibilities and praise effort.
